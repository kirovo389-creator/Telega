Telega local Telegram bypass transport
======================================

SOCKS5 to MTProto-over-WebSocket transport. Its compatible endpoint-routing
approach references the MIT-licensed Flowseal/tg-ws-proxy project:
https://github.com/Flowseal/tg-ws-proxy
